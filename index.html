    Rows copied —
    What is copied transactions, budgets, goals, customers, invoices, bills…

    A copy is sent automatically a couple of seconds after you add or change something, and once when the app loads. It needs a signed-in account and supabase/schema.sql to have been run.

    Open Supabase table editor
    One-way on purpose: SQLite is the working copy and Supabase is the copy. Editing rows in the Supabase table editor will not come back into the app — the next copy replaces them. Deleting your account removes these rows too.

    How the two modes differ

    What belongs where

    Individual

    • Personal income and expenses with personal categories
    • One monthly spending budget plus optional per-category budgets
    • Savings goals funded by contributions
    • Recurring expenses and upcoming expected payments
    • Monthly reports and CSV export

    Business

    • Revenue and business expenses with business categories
    • Customers and vendors
    • Invoices with line items, payments and derived status
    • Vendor bills and payables
    • Cash-flow and tax reports, CSV export

    Data never crosses modes: every row carries a workspace_id, and every API query is filtered by it.
