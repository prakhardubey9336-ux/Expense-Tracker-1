SmartSpend Your money, clearly organised.

Two books.
One clear picture.

Personal budgets, savings goals and recurring bills — plus invoices, customers and cash flow for a small business. Your account follows you; the money data still lives in SQLite on the machine running the server.

  • ✓ Your rows are private to your account (Row Level Security)
  • ✓ Individual and Business books never mix
  • ✓ Amounts stored in paise, so totals never drift

SmartSpend is a college mini project. Sign-in is real (Supabase Auth), but it is not a production security review — do not store real bank credentials here.
